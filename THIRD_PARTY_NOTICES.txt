Vendored runtime components

The Atelier bridge snapshot includes React 19.1.0 and ReactDOM 19.1.0,
including react.production.js, react-jsx-runtime.production.js and
react-dom.production.js. These components are not dependencies resolved by
this repository's pnpm lockfile. Their retained snapshot identity is recorded
in scripts/policy/vendored-runtime.json and included in the generated SBOM.
